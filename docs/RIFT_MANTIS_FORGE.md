# Rift Mantis — advanced candidate
Original non-humanoid rebuild of the existing rustpg:rift_mantis (72 HP, 9 base attack in the current definition). No asset borrowed from other mods. Six walking legs and two raptorial arms; each leg has two-link IK and tarsus orientation. Separate tripod stance/swing, segmented abdomen, serrated curved crystal blades, compound eyes, animated mandibles and antennae. Twelve clips, five authored attacks, transparent effect flipbooks, event/socket-based offline preview, target and bestiary portraits, optional boss bar skin without a portrait. Existing species and playable assets are unchanged. This is an offline candidate; no real Android FPS, server attacks, projectile entities, sound files, emissive renderer or in-game VFX are claimed. The workflow checks contact and forward direction twice (authoring solver and saved BBModel for hit events), pixel alpha, Rust compile/test, and stores all diagnostic reports including Blockbench warnings. Attack timings and balances remain provisional. The old Asset Studio workflow is not needed for this job; no Codespaces.
